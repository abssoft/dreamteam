# UI design

Review the composition within the skill's working-size scope. A local change covers the affected element and its immediate context; a new screen covers its composition. Treat an element as a meaningful control, field, message, or information group. Review repeated instances together when their purpose and behavior match.

## Composition

Start from the affected role, user task, and intended product outcome. For every affected element, settle:

1. **Purpose**: which task or outcome does it support? Orientation, understanding, feedback, and error prevention are valid purposes; use the known product context to establish the connection.
2. **Necessity**: what would the user lose without it? Does another element already serve that purpose?
3. **Placement**: at what point in the scenario is it needed, and beside which information or action?
4. **Visibility**: for which roles and states is it needed? Decide whether it stays visible or appears conditionally, and what makes it available or unavailable.
5. **Emphasis**: how important is it relative to its neighbours in that state? Choose the visual priority while keeping secondary content readable and actions discoverable; urgency and consequence can outweigh frequency of use.

Use these answers to keep, remove, combine, relocate, conditionally show, or change the emphasis of elements in the proposed solution. Keep agreed requirements and mockups, and existing behavior outside the requested change, as constraints; a material contradiction becomes an interview question within the existing budget and scope rules.

Check the composition as a whole: can the role complete the changed scenario, find the next action, and understand the result? Account for any missing control or feedback this reveals within the agreed scope.

The pass is complete when every affected element has a settled purpose, necessity, placement, visibility, and emphasis, and the composition leaves no unresolved material contradiction or missing step in the changed scenario.

## Output

Apply the check internally. Share material findings and unresolved choices through the skill's existing progress notes and interview. Put the resulting behavior in «Что дорабатываем» and the mockup when one is needed; put observable conditions in «Критерии приемки доработки». Describe conditions explicitly, such as when a bulk-action panel appears and disappears. Provide an element-by-element analysis table only on request. Preserve the existing PRD sections and handoff.
